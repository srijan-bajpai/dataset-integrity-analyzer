## Architectural Validation Summary

1. **C1 (Breadth / Unification)**: All six health dimensions write to and operate over the unified per-sample record ($D_4$), enabling the battery of 6 cross-dimensional confound tests.
2. **C2 (Depth / Downstream Impact)**: The 16-D bias descriptor vector integrates all dimensions and confound effect sizes to estimate expected accuracy drop ($\Delta \text{acc}$) and sub-group gap ($\Delta \text{gap}$) with prediction intervals and feature attributions. The ablation study in `experiments/c2_benchmark_results.json` empirically proves that withholding cross-dimensional confounds degrades predictive accuracy ($R^2$ drops from $0.8909$ to $0.7638$).
3. **Automated Verification**: The entire backend suite passes with 36 automated unit, integration, transformation, and security tests (`python -m pytest`).
